# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Product: SpendLens by Credex

---

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Startup founders and CTOs at Series A/B companies (10-100 employees) spending $500-$5,000/month on AI tools.

**Secondary:** Engineering managers at larger companies managing team AI budgets.

**Psychographic:** Technically literate, cost-conscious, skeptical of vendor lock-in, active on HN/Twitter.

---

## Launch Channels

### 1. Hacker News — Show HN
**Post:** "Show HN: SpendLens — Audit your AI tool spending in 3 minutes (free)"  
**Why:** HN audience is exactly the ICP. Technical founders who care about burn.  
**Expected:** 50-200 upvotes, 500-2,000 visitors on launch day.

### 2. X/Twitter
**Hook:** "We audited 50 startup AI stacks. The average team is wasting $2,400/year on AI tools they don't need. Here's what we found: [link]"  
**Format:** Thread with specific examples (Cursor Business vs Pro, Claude Max vs Pro)  
**Target accounts:** @levelsio, @paulg, @sama adjacent audience

### 3. Product Hunt
**Category:** Developer Tools / Finance  
**Launch day:** Tuesday (highest traffic)  
**Hunter:** Reach out to top hunters 2 weeks before  
**Goal:** Top 5 of the day

### 4. Founder communities
- Indie Hackers
- YC alumni Slack
- Lenny's Newsletter community
- SaaStr community

---

## Conversion Funnel

```
Cold visitor → Landing page → Start audit → Complete form → See results → Email capture → Consultation
     100%           60%           40%            30%            28%           8%              2%
```

**Key insight:** The results page is the conversion moment. The big green savings number is the hook.

---

## Viral Loop

1. User completes audit
2. Shares public link with team/CFO
3. Team member views shared audit
4. Team member starts their own audit
5. Repeat

**Amplifier:** "Share your audit" CTA on results page. Public share pages have full OG metadata for Twitter/LinkedIn previews.

---

## Lead Qualification

Leads are qualified by:
- Annual savings identified (>$500 = high intent)
- Team size (>10 = enterprise potential)
- Role (Founder/CTO/CFO = decision maker)

**Credex follow-up sequence:**
1. Immediate: Automated audit report email
2. Day 2: Personal email from Credex advisor if savings >$1,000/yr
3. Day 7: "Did you implement the recommendations?" follow-up

---

## Metrics to Track

- Audit completion rate (form start → results)
- Email capture rate (results → lead)
- Share rate (results → share link created)
- Consultation booking rate (lead → call booked)
- Average identified savings per audit
