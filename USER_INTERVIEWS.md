## Interview Template

**Duration:** 20-30 minutes  
**Format:** Video call or async Loom  
**Screener:** Startup with 5+ employees, using 2+ AI tools, spending >$200/month

### Questions

1. Walk me through your current AI tool stack. What do you use and why?
2. Do you know your exact monthly AI spend? How do you track it?
3. Have you ever audited or questioned your AI tool choices? What triggered it?
4. Have you ever switched AI tools to save money? What was the process?
5. Who in your company makes AI tool purchasing decisions?
6. What would make you trust a savings recommendation enough to act on it?
7. Would you share an audit result with your team? Your CFO?
